WHO WE HELP

If any of this sounds familiar, we should talk.

Rhemroyal works with a range of organisations at different stages. What they tend to have in common is ambition that has outpaced structure.

Startups & Founders

You have a genuine idea and the drive to build it, but no formal plan, structure or strategy behind it yet.

  • You're still explaining the idea differently every time someone asks
  • You haven't written a business plan, or the one you have feels theoretical
  • You're making big decisions without a sounding board

Emerging Organisations

You're past the idea stage — there's a team, some traction, maybe early clients — but the foundations were never formalised.

  • Roles and responsibilities are unclear or overlapping
  • Decisions rely on one or two people's memory, not documented process
  • Growth feels chaotic rather than deliberate

Growing Businesses & Teams

The business is working, but the systems that got you here are now creating friction, not removing it.

  • The team has grown faster than your processes
  • Meetings and reporting have multiplied without adding clarity
  • You need a growth roadmap, not just more hours in the week

Community & Social Impact Initiatives

Your mission is clear, but turning that mission into planned, measurable impact is the harder part.

  • You're volunteer-led or informally structured and want to formalise
  • You need an impact plan to support a funding or partnership conversation
  • You want a way to actually measure the difference you're making
BEING UPFRONT

When we might not be the right fit.

We'd rather tell you this now than after a consultation. Rhemroyal isn't a law firm, accountancy practice or regulated financial adviser — if you need formal legal, tax or regulated financial advice, we'll point you toward someone qualified to give it, and can work alongside them on the strategy and planning side.

We're also built for organisations that want an ongoing working relationship, not a one-off document with no support to implement it. If that's genuinely all you need, we're happy to say so.
