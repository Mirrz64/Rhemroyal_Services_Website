CASE STUDIES

How engagements like these typically play out.

Rhemroyal Services is a young consultancy, so the examples below are illustrative scenarios based on how our services are designed to work — not yet real client results. We'll replace them with real, named stories as soon as we have client permission to share them.

ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE

An early-stage social enterprise gets funding-ready

Situation: A community food initiative had a strong mission and loyal volunteers, but no formal business or impact plan — which was starting to block funding applications.

Approach: From Idea to Action programme, combining Business Planning and Social Development services over six weeks.

"We finally had a plan that matched how seriously we took the mission."

6 wksfrom first session to a funding-ready plan
3funding applications submitted using the new plan
1theory of change document the team still uses
ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE

A growing training company restructures around clarity

Situation: A 12-person training provider had grown quickly, but roles had blurred, deadlines were slipping, and decisions bottlenecked around the founder.

Approach: Organisational Development combined with the Organisation Growth Accelerator programme.

"We stopped firefighting and started running the business on purpose."

4clearly defined leadership roles introduced
90 daysto a working growth roadmap with quarterly milestones
1documented decision-making process replacing informal habits
ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE

A volunteer-led initiative starts measuring its impact

Situation: A community mentoring group had been running informally for two years and wanted to show funders and partners the difference it was making.

Approach: Social Impact Development programme, including a needs assessment and outcome measurement framework.

"For the first time we could actually show our numbers, not just tell our story."

1outcome measurement framework built from scratch
2new partnership conversations opened using the results
