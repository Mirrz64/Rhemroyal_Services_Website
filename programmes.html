PROGRAMMES

Structured support for organisations ready to move.

Where our services are flexible building blocks, our programmes are structured journeys — a defined starting point, a clear process, and a defined outcome.

START

From Idea to Action

For aspiring founders and early-stage organisations that need clarity, structure and a first practical roadmap.

You have an idea you believe in, but turning it into something you can actually run is a different skill from having it. This programme takes you from concept to a working first-90-days plan.

Who it's for

  • Aspiring founders yet to formalise their business
  • Early-stage organisations without a written plan
  • Anyone who has an idea but not yet a structure around it

What you'll walk away with

  • A clear vision and positioning statement
  • A practical first business plan
  • A 90-day action roadmap with concrete next steps

GROW

Organisation Growth Accelerator

For young organisations that have traction but are struggling with goals, systems, positioning or sustainable growth.

Early traction creates a new problem: the systems that worked for three people don't work for fifteen. This programme rebuilds your structure and goals to match the size you're actually operating at.

Who it's for

  • Organisations with real traction but rising internal friction
  • Teams that have outgrown informal ways of working
  • Leaders who need a clearer growth roadmap

What you'll walk away with

  • An organisational structure fit for your next stage
  • A goal-setting and KPI framework the whole team uses
  • A growth roadmap with quarterly milestones

IMPACT

Social Impact Development

For purpose-led initiatives seeking stronger planning, capacity and measurable community outcomes.

This programme is built for organisations whose success is measured in outcomes for people, not just revenue. We help you plan, build capacity, and show the impact you're already making.

Who it's for

  • Community and social initiatives at any stage
  • Volunteer-led groups formalising into structured organisations
  • Purpose-led teams preparing for funding or partnership conversations

What you'll walk away with

  • A theory of change and impact plan
  • Stronger operational capacity
  • An outcome measurement framework you can report against
